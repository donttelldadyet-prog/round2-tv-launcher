1 · Feel

Our feel comes from the logo: monochrome – white on black, or black on white. Thin, rounded lines; outlines, not fills; small round corners; wide letter-spacing. Colours are our product: each room's colour, and the few state colours. Nothing else is coloured – no gradients, glass or glow.

2 · Colour

Rooms own their colour. States are a small dot, never a coloured block.

3 · Room tiles – the main view

The six rooms are the screen. Inside a tile everything follows the room's colour. The big number is always a countdown with words (left · cleaning left · till arrival). The ring has one meaning: guests are in – it fills as their booking is used and says how long they booked.

Hover a tile, button, row or tab – everything here reacts the way it will in the apps. The live preview on the right lets you switch a tile's state and tune the motion.

4 · Quiet info strip

Everything that isn't a room sits underneath: columns split by thin lines, no boxes.

5 · Controls

Outlines until pressed or on. One solid button per screen – the main action.
